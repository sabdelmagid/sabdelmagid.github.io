My research aims to make generative AI safer by understanding how image and vision-language models cause harm and developing ways to measure and prevent it.

Research

My vision is safer generative AI that mitigates real-world harm.

But, we traditionally study one model on one task. In practice, a generator’s outputs are collected into datasets that train other models (scorers, classifiers, the next generator), and those models act back on the generator by filtering, finetuning, and evaluating it.

Harm from generative AI is produced by an ecosystem of models, data, and people, so safety has to be understood, evaluated, and controlled at the level of that ecosystem.

I’m always happy to collaborate, please reach out if this is of interest!
